Welcome to AnyDL

Download videos, music, images and files from almost any link. Share a link to AnyDL, pick what you want, and save it to Photos or Files.

Set up once

1
Install a-Shell and open it once The full a-Shell app from the App Store, not "a-Shell mini". AnyDL runs its downloader inside it.
2
Tap "Always Allow" The first few times, iOS asks whether AnyDL may use a-Shell and open Shortcuts. Always Allow keeps it from asking again.
3
Keep the name "AnyDL" a-Shell uses the name to hand results back to the shortcut.
4
Give the first download a minute It installs the download tools in a-Shell. After that, downloads start right away.

How to use it

Share a link to AnyDL from any app, or copy a link and run AnyDL.
Your download opens in a preview. Use the share button to save it, then tap Done: a-Shell shows one preview at a time.
Everything is also in Files › On My iPhone › a-Shell › AnyDL.
Settings in the menu: video quality, MP3 audio, and update checks.
